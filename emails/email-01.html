A Great Cloud

This Sunday, November 1, the Church celebrates All Saints Day—the men and women who ran the race before us and now behold the face of God. On Monday, November 2, All Souls Day, we pray for the faithful departed, entrusting our fathers, grandfathers, brothers, and friends to the mercy of Christ.

“Since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us run with perseverance the race that is set before us.”

Hebrews 12:1

Both days remind us that no man walks the road to heaven alone. That is the heart of brotherhood, and it is why we gather.
