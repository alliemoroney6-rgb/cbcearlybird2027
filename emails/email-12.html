Fourth Sunday of Advent — December 20 ADVENT WEEK 4
Love
Brothers,

A blessed Fourth Sunday of Advent to you and your family. Christmas is almost here.

This Sunday the Church sets Mary before us at the Annunciation. Love, in Scripture, is not a feeling — it's a yes. Mary gave hers without knowing how it would all unfold, and God was made flesh through it.

All four candles are lit now. Before the rush of the week ahead, what yes is God asking of you?
This Sunday's Gospel "Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord. May it be done to me according to your word." (Luke 1:26-38)
This Week's Challenge Do one hidden act of love for someone in your home this week — a chore, a note, an hour of undivided attention — and tell no one. Love that seeks no credit looks most like Christ's.
