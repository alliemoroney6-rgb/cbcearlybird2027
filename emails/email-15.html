Solemnity Of The Church MARY, MOTHER OF GOD January 1st
On January 1st, during the octave day of Christmas, the Church honors the Blessed Virgin Mary under her oldest and highest title: Theotokos, Mother of God. The Council of Ephesus affirmed this in 431 — because her Son Jesus is truly God, Mary is truly His Mother, not merely the mother of His human nature.

It is fitting that the Church opens each calendar year at Mary's feet. She said yes to God without reservation and carried Christ into the world. As we set our sights on the year ahead, we do well to ask her intercession for the same trust.
Practical Step Entrust your year to her Pray one decade of the rosary for your family and the year ahead. As you pray, entrust to Mary whatever you're carrying into January — a decision, a struggle, a hope for your household.
