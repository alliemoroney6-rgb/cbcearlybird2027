First Sunday of Advent — November 29 ADVENT WEEK 1
Hope
Brothers,

A blessed Advent to all! May this season bring abundant blessings to you and your family. May we keep our eyes fixed on the Lord and prepare well for the celebration of Christmas and the Incarnation.

Advent opens in near darkness — one small flame against three candles still unlit. That is the posture of hope: not certainty that everything "will be okay" or "work out", but a peace that the presence of God will be with us no matter what lies ahead.

This week, let one flame be enough. Hope doesn't require the whole wreath lit at once.
This Sunday's Gospel "Be watchful! Be alert! You do not know when the time will come." (Mark 13:33-37)
This Week's Challenge Set one alarm this week — same time every day — as a reminder to stop and pray for sixty seconds. Watchfulness starts as a habit before it becomes a virtue.
